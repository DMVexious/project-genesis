Controls

Pick a mode in the bar at the top of the world (or press 1, 2, 3). H shows this again.

👁 Watch 1

  • Drag, or WASD, to move around; scroll to zoom
  • Click a villager to follow them and see their stats, skills and things
  • Space pause · M minimap · , . replay
  • Click someone to hear what they're doing (🔊)

⚡ God 2

  • Click a villager to open the god menu
  • ⚡ Smite them with lightning, ❤️ heal, 🍖 feed
  • Type in the box to speak from the sky: they always answer
  • 🎮 Become them to take over their body

🎮 Play 3

  • Click a villager to become them
  • Click the ground to walk; click a tree or bush to chop or pick it; WASD take steps
  • Click someone to talk (type anything), hit, trade, give or court; they answer
  • When someone talks to you, answer in the box at the bottom
  • The bottom bar has what you can do where you stand · Esc leaves the body
